Rich Heritage of India

Explore India's magnificent monuments and ancient architecture.

Taj Mahal

A world-famous white marble monument in Agra, admired for its beautiful architecture.

Hawa Mahal

Jaipur's famous Palace of Winds is known for its distinctive façade and small windows.

Red Fort

A historic Delhi landmark reflecting India's rich Mughal-era architecture.

Ancient Temples

Discover remarkable stone carvings, intricate designs, and centuries-old traditions.

Festivals of India

Celebrate the colours, customs, and happiness of Indian festivals.

Holi

The festival of colours celebrates friendship, happiness, and the arrival of spring.

Diwali

The festival of lights brings families together with lamps, sweets, and decorations.

Onam

Kerala's harvest festival features floral designs, traditional feasts, and cultural events.

Ganesh Chaturthi

A festival dedicated to Lord Ganesha, celebrated with prayers and rituals to seek his blessings.

Art & Dance of India

Discover the creativity and artistic traditions of India's diverse cultures.

Bharatanatyam

A classical dance known for expressive gestures, rhythmic movements, and traditional costumes.

Kathakali

Kerala's classical dance-drama is famous for elaborate makeup, colourful costumes, and storytelling.

Traditional Paintings

Explore Indian art styles such as Madhubani, Warli, and Pattachitra.

Handicrafts

Discover handmade pottery, woven textiles, woodwork, and crafts created by skilled artisans.

Flavours of India

Every region has its own delicious story to tell.

South India

Dosa, Idli, Appam and Sadhya. Enjoy flavours and traditional dishes from southern states.

North India

Chole Bhature, Paratha and Kulcha. Discover rich curries, breads, and aromatic spices.

West India

Dhokla, Thepla and Pav Bhaji. Taste the colourful snacks and specialities of western India.

East India

Enjoy regional rice dishes, fish specialities, sweets, and traditional Bengali cuisine.

Explore India's Beauty

From green landscapes to magnificent monuments.

Kerala

Explore peaceful backwaters, green hills, beaches, and beautiful natural landscapes.

Rajasthan

Discover royal palaces, colourful cities, grand forts, and desert traditions.

Punjab

Experience vibrant folk dances, welcoming traditions, fertile fields, and Punjabi cuisine.

Assam

Discover lush tea gardens, the Brahmaputra River, wildlife, and traditional Bihu celebrations.
